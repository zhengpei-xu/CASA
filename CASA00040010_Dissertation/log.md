## 2026-03-12 — Methodology Development

**Type:** `Writing` | **Focus:** LTS Framework Adaptation

### Progress
Drafted the core rationale for the UK-adapted LTS framework:

**Key argument:** Existing LTS frameworks were developed for the North American road environment and cannot be applied directly to UK conditions. The proposed enhancement replaces American-origin thresholds with design standards from **LTN 1/20** — England's national cycling infrastructure guidance — producing a framework that is directly grounded in the standards against which West Midlands cycling schemes will be assessed.

### Decisions Made
- LTN 1/20 thresholds will replace North American equivalents as the primary source of design standards
- Framework will remain road-segment based (consistent with original LTS methodology)

### Still Unresolved
- Intersection stress: how to map LTN 1/20 guidance onto intersection-level indicators
- Weighted aggregation vs. parallel dimensions — final aggregation logic not yet decided

### Next Steps
- [ ] Build indicator crosswalk table: LTS indicators ↔ LTN 1/20 equivalents
- [ ] Review Furth & Nixon (2016) intersection stress methodology
